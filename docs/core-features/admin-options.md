# Change Password

## Overview

Change Password All users can change their own passwords (this does not apply to FLOWCALInstant Login users). You will need to log in with your existing password to update this information.

## Key Characteristics

- Passwords must conform to the requirements established for your Oracle profile by your database administrator.
- To change your password: Go to Settings , and select Change Password .
- Enter your old password.
- Passwords must contain a minimum of three characters.
- Enter your new password.
- Enter the password again to confirm the change.
