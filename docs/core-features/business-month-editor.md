# Business Month Editor

## Overview

Business Month Editor The Current Business Month is usually defined as the open production month currently being processed. The business month is used in areas of such as PPAs.

## Key Characteristics

- This business month concept also allows users to explicitly set the current business month for a given system within the application.
- By explicitly setting a business month, there could be one or more open production months, but the user has control over which month is the current business month.
- In addition, some data has the current business month recorded as part of the audit trail.
- Each FLOWCALsystem is set to use an explicit business month.
- For liquid meters, Tickets now have an explicitly recorded Business Month as part of the audit trail.
- Tickets that belong to a Location use the Location's System to establish the Business Month.
