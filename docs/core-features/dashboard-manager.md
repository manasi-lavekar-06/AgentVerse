# Arranging and Customizing Tiles

## Overview

Arranging and Customizing Tiles The quick launch tiles can be arranged on the dashboard in any order. Simply drag and drop a tile into a new location.

## Key Characteristics

- While you are moving the tile, it will display as semi-transparent.
- Arrange tiles starting at the top left corner and working your way down, two columns at a time.
- Tile Groups Groups are denoted by increased space between tiles or tools.
- To create a new group of tiles or tools: Click the desired tile or tool and drag it to an open area on the right side of the dashboard.
- This will create a new group area where other items can be added.
- Add more items to the group as needed.
