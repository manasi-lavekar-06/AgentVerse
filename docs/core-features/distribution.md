# Gas Distribution and End Users

Describes the final stage of natural gas flow from pipeline to end users. Explains distribution systems, various customer categories including residential, commercial, and industrial, and how measurement occurs at delivery points.

## Distribution Systems and Companies

Natural gas distribution systems transport gas from transmission pipelines to end users. Distribution companies operate these local systems and are responsible for delivering gas to customers. Examples include CenterPoint Energy, Atmos Energy, and others. Distribution companies own and operate the infrastructure from the city gate (where gas enters from transmission pipelines) through local distribution networks to individual customer connections.

- Distribution companies operate local gas delivery networks.
- Gas flows from transmission pipelines to distribution systems.
- Distribution infrastructure extends from city gates to customers.
- Major distributors include CenterPoint Energy, Atmos Energy, and others.
- Distribution companies manage local pipeline infrastructure.

## Measurement at Distribution Points

Measurement occurs at various points in the distribution system where gas is delivered to or transferred between customers. City gates represent delivery points where transmission company gas enters distribution company systems. At these gates and throughout the distribution system, meters measure the volume of gas being delivered, ensuring accurate billing and ownership accounting. Measurement points occur whenever there is a change in ownership or at regulatory checkpoints.

- City gate meters measure gas entering distribution systems.
- Measurement ensures accurate billing to end users.
- Meters track ownership transfers between companies.
- Distribution point meters measure gas delivered to customers.
- Regulatory requirements mandate measurement at key points.

## End User Categories and Usage

Natural gas is delivered to diverse end-user categories. Residential customers include individual homes where gas is used for heating and appliances. Commercial customers include businesses that use gas for operations. Industrial customers include large-scale operations such as agricultural drying operations that require significant gas volumes. Some very large industrial users may receive gas directly from transmission pipelines rather than through distribution networks. City gate deliveries serve municipal distribution systems that deliver gas to residential and commercial end users.

- Residential users: individual homes for heating and appliances.
- Commercial users: businesses for heating and operations.
- Industrial users: large operations requiring high gas volumes.
- Agricultural operations: use gas for crop drying.
- City gates: deliver gas to municipal distribution systems.
- End-user categories determine distribution requirements.
