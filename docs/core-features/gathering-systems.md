# Gathering Systems

Explains how gathering systems collect and consolidate natural gas from multiple production wells into larger pipelines for transport. Describes the role of gathering companies, quality management, and the transition from well-site production to major pipeline infrastructure.

## Purpose of Gathering Systems

A gathering system collects natural gas from multiple production wells and consolidates it into a larger pipe for transportation. Natural gas cannot be trucked as a liquid and must be transported via pipeline. Gathering systems bridge the gap between individual well sites and major transmission pipelines. Multiple wells feed into progressively larger pipes that combine and direct the gas stream toward processing facilities and major pipeline systems.

- Gathering systems collect gas from multiple production wells.
- Gas consolidates from smaller to larger pipes for transport.
- Natural gas requires pipeline transport (cannot be trucked).
- Gathering systems connect well sites to major pipelines.
- A gathering system serves as a collection and consolidation network.

## Gathering Companies and Ownership

Gathering systems are operated by specialized gathering companies that are separate from the production companies. These companies may take ownership of the gas after it exits the well meters, or they may be contracted to simply transport and manage the flow. Gathering companies are paid for their services and manage the quality and movement of gas through their system. At the point where gas enters a gathering system, ownership may transfer from the production company to the gathering company, creating a measurement and accounting point.

- Gathering systems are operated by specialized gathering companies.
- Gathering companies may own the gas or work on a service basis.
- Ownership transfers may occur at the gathering system entry point.
- Gathering companies manage quality and gas movement.
- Measurement points track ownership transfers for accounting.

## Gathering and Pipeline Quality Requirements

As gas moves through gathering systems toward major pipelines, it must meet quality specifications. Pipelines have strict requirements for gas quality including BTU content, water content, and hydrogen sulfide levels. If gas does not meet pipeline A's specifications, it may need to be routed to pipeline B, treated to improve quality, or processed further. Gathering systems manage this quality control and routing based on pipeline requirements and current specifications.

- Pipelines have quality requirements: BTU content, water, hydrogen sulfide levels.
- Gathering systems ensure gas meets pipeline specifications.
- Non-conforming gas may be routed to alternative pipelines.
- Gas may require additional treatment before pipeline acceptance.
- Quality management is integral to gathering system operations.
