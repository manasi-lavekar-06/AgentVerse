# About Sources

## Overview

About Sources Sources represent flowing product composition at strategic points in a pipeline. Product samples may be drawn into a sample cylinder for local or lab analysis, or continuously drawn and analyzed by an online chromatograph.

## Key Characteristics

- In FLOWCAL, these types of sources are called "sample analysis" and "chromatograph" sources.
- In FLOWCAL Gas, an online chromatograph may be connected to the EFM, which will perform weighted averaging of the analyses.
- This is called "RTU average".
- Source Types Sample Analysis Sample analyses, or samples, are drawn locally either on a periodic "spot" basis or on a continuous "composite" basis.
- Sample frequencies typically range from monthly to annual.
- Chromatograph Online chromatographs are connected to the pipeline and continuously flow a small amount of product through a transfer line.
