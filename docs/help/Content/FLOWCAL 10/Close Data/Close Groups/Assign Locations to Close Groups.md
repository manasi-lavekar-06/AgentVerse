# <span id="aanchor270"></span> Assign Locations to Close Groups

After assigning a meter or group of meters to a location, you can assign that location to a close group. You can also assign locations to close groups in bulk from the Bulk Changes screen.

<div class="procedure">

To assign a location to a close group:

1.  Go to **Setup** \> **Location** \> **Editor**.
2.  Select a location, and click the **Edit** button.
3.  Select the **Close Group** tab.
4.  Double-click on a row in the table to open the Select Close Group screen.
5.  Select a close group.
6.  Enter a start and end month and year.
7.  Click **OK** to return to the Location Editor.
8.  Click **Save** to apply the changes.

</div>

You can also use the Close Group tab to assign a location to more than one close group during different time periods.

A Close Group tab is available in the Location Editor for liquids to allow you to close tickets associated with the location. For gas, if you associate a location with a close group and close it, all the meters within the location will be closed automatically.
