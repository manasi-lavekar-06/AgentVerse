# <span id="aanchor390"></span> View Close Groups

You may need to see a list of all close groups that have been created in FLOWCAL, or a list of all close groups assigned to a meter.

To view all available close groups: Go to **Setup** \> **Close Groups**.

To view the close groups assigned to a meter: Go to the Meter Editor, and click button next to the Close Groups drop-down on the General tab, or go to **Tools** \> **Manage Close Groups**.
