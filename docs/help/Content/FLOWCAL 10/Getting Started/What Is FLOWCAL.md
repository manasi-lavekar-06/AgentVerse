# What Is FLOWCAL?

FLOWCAL Enterprise was developed as a corporate solution for demanding system requirements, minimizing the efforts required for managing a complex measurement department. Throughout the measurement industry, companies in the exploration and production, midstream, pipeline, and local distribution sectors use this integrated system to validate, balance, store, and report gas and liquid measurement data.

## Consolidation

The FLOWCAL system is a comprehensive, integrated software package that manages electronic gas and liquid measurement data from the field to the corporate accounting system. FLOWCAL displays all your data in one place, providing a single location for you to review and edit information, and a complete audit trail compliant with all industry standards.

## Data Quality

FLOWCAL interfaces with all major SCADA systems, polling engines, and flow computer manufacturers to automate the import process from your field devices. With more than 150 configurable validation set points, FLOWCAL identifies even the smallest flow anomalies out of massive amounts of data, and alerts the measurement team of potential errors.

Additionally, FLOWCAL’s advanced system balancing and inventory management feature displays gain/loss data so you can put those dollars back where they belong.

## Distribution Schedules

FLOWCAL offers a built-in report scheduler so that you can automatically distribute tickets, volume statements and other reports to management, customers or accounting without lifting a finger.
