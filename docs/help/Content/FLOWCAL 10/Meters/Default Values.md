# <span id="aanchor280"></span> View Meter Data

Once a meter has been created in FLOWCAL, you can view general information about the meter or other meter data, such as flow data.

#### General Information

General meter information, such as custom fields, validations and import options, are viewable from the [Meter Editor](Meter%20Editor/Meter%20Editor.md).

#### Flow Data

Flow data, meter characteristics, and meter analysis are available in the Volume Editor. This data is rolled up into hourly, daily and monthly records, which are then available in the Meter/Location Rollup Viewer. This rolled data is primarily used for reporting externally and internally to other departments and downstream systems.

Other screens throughout the application, such as the Ad Hoc Data Review or Graph Tool screens, contain additional views of meter data. You can also run any of the standard FLOWCAL reports or exports to review or extract specific meter data.
