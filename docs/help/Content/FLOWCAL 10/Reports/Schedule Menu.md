## Schedule Menu

The following options are available on the Schedule menu on the Standard Reports screen:

- **Scheduler** - Open the Schedule Editor to edit, delete, or create report or export schedules. (See: [Report/Export Scheduling](Report%20Scheduler/Scheduling.md))
- **Status** - Open the Export and Report Scheduler Information screen to view the status of scheduled reports and/or exports. (See: [Status](Report%20Scheduler/Status.md))
- **Configuration** - Open the Report Scheduler Configuration screen to configure your SMTP server or output printers. (See: [Configure Report/Export Scheduling](Report%20Scheduler/Configure%20Report-Export%20Scheduling.md))
