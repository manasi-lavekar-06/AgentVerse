## Main Tab

The Main tab contains measurement data needed to calculate and validate volume and mass as well as the volume and mass for tickets. The available fields will vary by ticket type and fluid service (hydrocarbon or non-hydrocarbon). For instance: Scale tickets include Weight In, Weight Out, and New Weight fields, whereas Tank Gauge Level tickets include Tank Level and Free Water Level fields.
