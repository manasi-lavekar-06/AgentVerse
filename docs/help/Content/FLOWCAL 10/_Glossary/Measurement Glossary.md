# <span id="aanchor288"></span> Measurement Glossary

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor1" class="GlossaryPageLink">Absolute Pressure</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  Atmospheric Pressure + Gauge Pressure

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor2" class="GlossaryPageLink">ACBI</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  Automatically Created Batch Upon Import

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor3" class="GlossaryPageLink">AGA-7 Calculation Method</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  American Gas Association method for calculating gas flow for a linear meter

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor4" class="GlossaryPageLink">Ambient Conditions</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  The conditions (pressure, temperature, humidity, etc.) of the medium surrounding an object such as the case of a meter, instrument, transducer, etc.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor5" class="GlossaryPageLink">Analysis Reports</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  Analysis reports allow the user to identify analysis information that has been applied to a meter

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor6" class="GlossaryPageLink">AP0</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  Atmospheric Pressure Zero

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor7" class="GlossaryPageLink">API Gravity</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  A term used to express the relative density of petroleum liquids. API gravity is measured by a hydrometer instrument having a scale graduated in degrees API.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor8" class="GlossaryPageLink">Application Process</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  The application process provides security options that affect all processes across the FLOWCAL application

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor9" class="GlossaryPageLink">Atmospheric Pressure</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  The pressure exerted by the weight of the atmosphere at the meter’s location. At sea level, the pressure is approximately 14.7 psi (101 kpa), often referred to as 1 atmosphere

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor10" class="GlossaryPageLink">Auto Rescan</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  Auto Rescan is a service that allows FLOWCAL check for missing data on meters and quality sources.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor11" class="GlossaryPageLink">Base Conditions</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  The conditions of temperature and pressure to which measured volumes are to be corrected. Defined in FLOWCAL as the system pressure and temperature base.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor12" class="GlossaryPageLink">Base Density</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  Determined from the density at flowing conditions by applying the density meter factor (DMF) to account for densitometer inaccuracies and then accounting for the temperature and pressure of the density measurement not being at base temperature and pressure

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor13" class="GlossaryPageLink">Batch</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  Used in liquids only. Volume of liquid product pass through a meter over a period of time. Accumulations of records taken in either hourly, daily, 15 minute or possibly other data resolutions.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor14" class="GlossaryPageLink">Batch Meter</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  Used in liquids only. Batch meters represent meter measured product flow. It contains continuous records of a meter’s options.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor15" class="GlossaryPageLink">Batch Schedule</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  Set schedule for automatically creating batches for liquid meters that deliver batches on a known schedule

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor16" class="GlossaryPageLink">Bill of Lading</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  A document by which a carrier acknowledges having received in good order and condition (or the reverse) certain specified goods consigned to them by some particular shipper and binds them to deliver those good in similar condition to the consignees of the shippers.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor17" class="GlossaryPageLink">Blow Down Ticket</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  Blow down tickets serve to indicate the quantity of product lost by venting.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor18" class="GlossaryPageLink">Business Month</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  The business month is the first month after the last close. If the last month closed is April and the current date is June 10, the business month is May (last month after the close in April)

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor19" class="GlossaryPageLink">Capacity Table</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  A table showing the capacities or volumes of a tank for various liquid levels measured from the reference gauge point; also known as a strapping table, tank capacity table or calibration table.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor20" class="GlossaryPageLink">Cell Editing</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  An option found in the Volume Editor that allows the user to edit more than one value for multiple records at a time in a spreadsheet-like format. This type of editing can only be done of flowing parameters (pressure, temperature, indicated volume, etc.).

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor21" class="GlossaryPageLink">CFX File</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  A proprietary binary file format used to bring meter flow data and configuration information into FLOWCAL.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor22" class="GlossaryPageLink">Close Group</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  A date-effective group of meters that allows the user to close meters in bulk. Meters can only belong to one close group at a time.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor23" class="GlossaryPageLink">Combined Correction Factor (CCF)</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  Factor that can be multiplied against the indicated volume to get the Gross Standard Volume (GSV). This factor takes all factors that need to be applied to take the volume measurement at flowing conditions to get an accurate volume at base temperature and pressure.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor24" class="GlossaryPageLink">Comparison Location</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  A location use to compare two measurement quantities (meters and/or locations).

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor25" class="GlossaryPageLink">Compressibility</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  The ratio of actual volume of gas at a given temperature and pressure to the volume of gas when calculated by the ideal gas law.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor26" class="GlossaryPageLink">Compressor</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  a piece of equipment which helps transport product from one location to another by compressing the product entering the compressor and then discharging that product at a higher pressure. Discharging the pressurized product causes it to move down the pipeline.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor27" class="GlossaryPageLink">Consignee</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  Party (usually a seller) who delivers a consignment to a carrier for transporting to a consignee (usually a buyer) named in the transportation documents. Ownership (title) of goods remains with the consignor until the consignee pays for them in full.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor28" class="GlossaryPageLink">Contractual Limits</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  Regulatory requirements defined in a tariff and/or operational agreements, maintenance agreements, and standard operating procedures and include items such as test frequency and sample frequency.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor29" class="GlossaryPageLink">Correction for Pressure of the Liquid (CPL)</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  Factor calculated from the products calculation table. The correction to be applied to the indicated volume to correct from flowing pressure to base pressure.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor30" class="GlossaryPageLink">Correction for Sediment & Water (CSW)</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  Factor to account for part of the product being measured as sediment and water. CSW will be 1 for refined products such as propane and gasoline. CSW will be less than 1.0 for products such a crude oil.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor31" class="GlossaryPageLink">Correction for Temperature and Pressure of Liquid (CTPL)</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  Factor calculated based on the product’s calculation table or by multiplying CTL and CPL together. CTPL is the correction to be applied to the indicated volume to correct from flowing temperature and pressure to base temperature and pressure.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor32" class="GlossaryPageLink">Correction for Temperature of Liquid (CTL)</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  Factor calculated based on product’s calculation table. The correction is applied to the indicated volume to correct from flowing temperature to the base temperature.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor33" class="GlossaryPageLink">Corresponding States Liquid Density (COSTALD)</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  COSTALD uses an equation of state to calculate density from composition.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor34" class="GlossaryPageLink">Cricondentherm</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  The highest temperature at any pressure at which hydrocarbons will condense out.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor35" class="GlossaryPageLink">Current Period Adjustment</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  Created on meter batches when a prove is entered in the Volume Editor prior to the month being closed

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor36" class="GlossaryPageLink">Datetime Effective</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  Datetime effective records have a defined start date and time and a defined end date and time.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor37" class="GlossaryPageLink">Density Meter Factor (DMF)</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  Correction factor to take uncorrected density to a corrected observed density. The factor is calculated by using a pycnometer ball to calculate a fluid’s density and then dividing that density with the density reported by the online densitometer for the same measurement. This is also called pyc factor or densitometer factor.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor38" class="GlossaryPageLink">Differential Pressure</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  The drop in pressure measured across an orifice plate or a cone. The amount of pressure drop is used to calculate the volume of gas or liquid flowing through a pipe.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor39" class="GlossaryPageLink">Dynamic List</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  A list that is based on a query that the user creates. This list will automatically be built based on the query selected.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor40" class="GlossaryPageLink">Edit Approval Limits</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  The maximum amount by which volume, energy, gross standard volume, or mass values can be changed through manual edits without requiring approval. Manual edits that exceed this limit will need to be approved before the edits are applied.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor41" class="GlossaryPageLink">Edit Limit</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  The maximum amount of change to volume, energy, GSV or mass that can be made by manual edits before edits require approval.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor42" class="GlossaryPageLink">Electronic Flow Measurement (EFM)</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  An electronic flow computer records the flowing parameters such as pressure, temperature and pulses and then calculates the volume.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor43" class="GlossaryPageLink">Equilibrium Vapor Pressure (EVP)</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  The pressure at a given temperature at which a fluid will transition from a liquid to a gas. Above the EVP the fluid is a liquid, below the EVP the fluid is a gas.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor44" class="GlossaryPageLink">Exception</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  A notification or flag created when suspect data for a meter, location or quality source is imported or added as an edit.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor45" class="GlossaryPageLink">Expert System</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  Functionality calculates Set Points and validates data based upon historical data using statistical analysis.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor46" class="GlossaryPageLink">GMS Date</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  The gas measurement system day on which a database record was created or last modified

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor47" class="GlossaryPageLink">Inventory Location</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  Inventory locations, such as underground sotrage caverns, are used to track storage amounts. An initial amount is added to or subtracted from the inventory. The results can then be viewed and reported in FLOWCAL.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor48" class="GlossaryPageLink">Keep Whole</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  Volume, energy, and mass for meters that are "kept whole" in the allocation process will not be adjusted to compensate for a surplus or deficit.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor49" class="GlossaryPageLink">Products</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  Types of fluid being measured, such as crude oil, natural gasoline, or propane

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor50" class="GlossaryPageLink">Sample ID</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  The Sample ID is unique across all standalone samples in a FLOWCAL database. This is in contrast to liquid quality source samples, whose unique identification is the LQ number and sample date. The Sample ID can contain up to 25 characters and is required for all standalone samples.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor51" class="GlossaryPageLink">SCADA</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor52" class="GlossaryPageLink">Source</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  The basis of gas or liquid quality analysis as defined by the user; a source could be a physical sample or online chromatograph; it is a representative sample either manually entered or imported into FLOWCAL and utilized to calculate volumes, mass, components, and gas equivalent values

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor53" class="GlossaryPageLink">TFX File</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  A proprietary binary file format used to import volume and accounting information from run tickets and gauge tickets (pipelines, LACTs, trucks, rail cars, marine vessels, or tank tickets) into FLOWCAL.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor54" class="GlossaryPageLink">Ticket</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  A measurement of data at a single point in time. A ticket can have a start date and an end date, but FLOWCAL treats it as a single data point.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor55" class="GlossaryPageLink">Useful Outlets/Inlets</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  Useful outlets include sales stations, storage, and product flowing downstream. Non-useful outlets include fuel, flare, and blowdown.

  </div>

- <div class="GlossaryPageTerm">

  <span id="3068130284_anchor56" class="GlossaryPageLink">WP0</span>

  </div>

  <div class="GlossaryPageDefinition">

  Working Pressure Zero

  </div>
