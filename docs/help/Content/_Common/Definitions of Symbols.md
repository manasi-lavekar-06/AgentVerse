### <span id="kanchor895"></span>Definitions of Symbols

The information in this section has been duplicated from Section 4.2 of the *API Manual of Petroleum Measurement Standards; Chapter 12.2 Part 3; First Edition; October 1998*. Additional symbols not defined in Ch.12.2 have been added from the *API Manual of Petroleum Measurement Standards; Chapter 5.6; First Edition; October 2002* to define mass proving symbols.

A combination of upper and lower case notation is used for symbols and formulas in this publication. Subscripted notation is often difficult to use in word-processed documents and therefore has not been used in this publication. Upper case notation is usually preferred for computer programming and other documents as appropriate.

Symbols have been defined to aid in clarity and specificity of the mathematical treatments. For example:

- CTL = Correction for Temperature on the Liquid
- GSV = Gross Standard Volume
- CPS = Correction for Pressure on Steel.

In many cases, the symbols are followed by additional letters to help clarify meaning and application. Examples include:

- “m” refers to the meter (as in CTLm)
- “p” refers to the meter prover (as in GSVp)
- “b” refers to base conditions (as in RHOb)
- “obs” refers to observed conditions (as in RHOobs)
- “avg” refers to the average (mean) of the readings \[as in Tp(avg)\]

**Note:** Additional letters may be in use as well.
